Why Students Worry About Attendance?

Let's be honest — sometimes you miss a few classes because of fever, travel, festivals, or simply "one extra day at home". Then suddenly your teacher announces: "Attendance list will be checked before exams."

💡 StudentCalc Tip

Don't keep attendance exactly at 75%. Always keep a small buffer like 77%–80% so you stay safe even if you miss a few more classes later.

What is 75 Percent Attendance?

75 percent attendance means a student must attend at least 75% of total classes conducted to remain eligible for examinations. This rule is common in many schools, colleges, and universities in India.

Some colleges keep stricter rules like 80%, while a few may allow 70%. So always check your official notice — but 75% is the most common minimum.

How to Calculate 75% Attendance

Attendance Percentage = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

If your answer is 75% or above, you're typically eligible for exams. Our calculator also tells you how many classes you can miss or must attend to reach the target.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many classes can I miss? ↓

You can miss up to 25% of total classes. Example: for 100 total classes, you can miss 25 and attend 75.

What if my attendance is below 75%? ↓

You'll need to attend continuously to recover. Some colleges may allow "extra classes" or assignments, but most will warn or stop exam entry if attendance is very low.

Do all colleges follow 75% rule? ↓

Not all. Many colleges follow 75%, some use 80%, and some allow 70%. Always check your college circular or department notice.

Should I keep my attendance exactly 75%? ↓

No 😄 keep some buffer like 77%–80%. Because sometimes classes increase suddenly and attendance drops automatically.
