What is Age Calculator?

An age calculator determines your exact age in years, months, and days from your date of birth to the current date. It accounts for leap years and varying month lengths for precise results.

How Age is Calculated

Age = Current Date - Birth Date

The difference is broken down into years, months, and remaining days for a complete picture of your age.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to calculate age manually? ↓

Subtract birth year from current year for age in years. For exact age, subtract birth month/day from current month/day. If birth month > current month, subtract 1 from year and add 12 months.

Does this calculator consider leap years? ↓

Yes! Our age calculator automatically accounts for leap years (February 29) in its calculations for perfect accuracy.

Can I calculate age for a past date? ↓

Yes, use the "Calculate as of" field to find age on any specific date — past, present, or future.
