About Me

I'm a student just like you. I've struggled with low attendance, distractions, and the endless cycle of "I'll study tomorrow." That's exactly why I built StudentCalc — because I needed these tools myself.

When my laptop charger broke and I gave my phone to my mom, I accidentally discovered the power of analog studying. No fancy apps. No productivity hacks that cost money. Just paper, pen, and a brain that's more capable than we give it credit for.

Through this blog, I share what actually works — not theoretical advice, but things I've tested on myself. Some worked. Some failed spectacularly. I write about both.

When I'm not studying or writing, you'll find me reading, attempting to cook, or convincing myself that tomorrow I'll finally wake up at 6 AM.
