What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the average of grade points earned across all subjects. It's used by CBSE and many universities to represent overall academic performance on a 10-point scale.

The Conversion Formula

Percentage = CGPA × 9.5

This is the standard CBSE formula. Some universities use ×10 or other multipliers. Our calculator supports multiple formats.

CBSE CGPA to Percentage

CBSE uses the formula: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. This is the official formula used for Class 10 and Class 12 results.

Example: 8.8 CGPA × 9.5 = 83.6%

Frequently Asked Questions

How to convert CGPA to percentage? ↓

Multiply your CGPA by 9.5. Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. This is the standard CBSE formula. For example, 8.4 CGPA = 8.4 × 9.5 = 79.8%.

What is the formula for CGPA to percentage in CBSE? ↓

CBSE uses: Percentage = CGPA × 9.5. This formula was derived by CBSE after analyzing the previous five years' results.

Is CGPA to percentage calculator accurate? ↓

Yes — it uses the standard academic formula accepted by CBSE and most universities. All calculations happen in your browser for accuracy.

What is a good CGPA? ↓

A CGPA above 8.0 (76% and above) is considered good. Above 9.0 (85.5% and above) is excellent and often qualifies for academic awards.

How to calculate percentage from CGPA for different universities? ↓

Different universities use different multipliers. Use our calculator's formula selector to choose between CBSE (×9.5), Mumbai University (×7.5), or Standard (×10).
