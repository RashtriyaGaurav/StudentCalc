What is marks percentage?

Marks percentage expresses a student's score as a number out of 100. It's the universal standard used in report cards, board results, college admissions, and scholarship eligibility across India.

The formula

( Obtained ÷ Total ) × 100

Universally accepted by CBSE, ICSE, and all state boards. StudentCalc uses exactly this formula — no rounding surprises.
