What is JEE Percentile & How It Works?

JEE Main percentile is a normalized score that indicates the percentage of candidates who scored equal to or below you. Unlike raw marks, percentile depends on the performance of all test-takers across shifts. For 2026, around 10–12 lakh students appear. Our predictor uses marks vs percentile mapping derived from last years’ trends and adjusts based on shift difficulty (easy/hard).

How Rank Is Calculated?

Formula: Rank = ((100 - Percentile) × Total Candidates) / 100. With total candidates ≈ 10,00,000. For instance, 95 percentile gives rank ~50,000. Our tool factors category (General/OBC/SC/ST) and shift normalization for better college range prediction. College ranges are based on JoSAA 2025 cutoffs.

Frequently Asked Questions (JEE Percentile & Rank)

How is JEE percentile calculated officially?

NTA calculates percentile using normalization formula based on multi-session papers. Your percentile indicates % of candidates who scored ≤ your marks. Higher raw marks = higher percentile generally. Our tool simulates that mapping with shift difficulty.

Marks vs percentile relation — is it linear?

No, it's exponential at top ranges. A jump from 250 to 270 can increase percentile from 99.5 to 99.95, while from 100 to 120 moves from 85 to 92. Our internal JS mapping uses piecewise realistic segments.

What rank will I get at 98.5 percentile?

Assuming 10 lakh candidates, rank = ((100-98.5)*1000000)/100 = 15,000. For General category, expect NITs like Surathkal / Trichy lower branches; for OBC/SC/ST rank improves further.

Is this JEE percentile predictor accurate for 2026?

We use historical trend + shift adjustment algorithm. While final NTA data may cause minor variation, our tool is benchmarked against last 3 years' JEE Main results, providing reliable estimates within ±2 percentile margin.

How can I improve my JEE rank in 2 months?

Focus on revision, high-scoring chapters, and daily full-length tests. Analyse weak topics, avoid silly mistakes, and maintain consistency. Use our predictor to set goal marks.
