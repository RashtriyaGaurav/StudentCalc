What is Pomodoro?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method that uses 25-minute focused work sessions followed by 5-minute breaks to boost productivity and reduce burnout. Developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s.

Why It Works

Short, focused bursts prevent mental fatigue, maintain high concentration, and make large tasks feel more manageable. Perfect for studying, coding, writing, or any demanding cognitive work.

  • • Remove distractions — put your phone away during focus sessions

  • • After 4 pomodoros, take a longer 15-20 minute break
  • • Track your sessions to see your productivity grow

Frequently Asked Questions

How many pomodoros should I do per day? ↓

For most students, 6-8 pomodoros (3-4 hours of focused study) per day is effective. Start with 4 pomodoros and gradually increase as your focus improves.

What should I do during breaks? ↓

Step away from your screen, stretch, walk around, hydrate, or do quick breathing exercises. Avoid social media during breaks as it can disrupt your focus rhythm.

Can I customize the timer duration? ↓

Currently, the timer uses the standard 25/5 Pomodoro intervals. Many studies show this is the optimal balance for focus and rest. Future updates may include customization.
