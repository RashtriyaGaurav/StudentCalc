Our commitment to your privacy

At StudentCalc.online, accessible at https://studentcalc.online, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. We built StudentCalc to be genuinely useful to students — not to harvest data.

Information we collect

StudentCalc does not collect any personal information unless you voluntarily provide it. This may include:

  • →Your name and email address, only if you contact us via our contact form.
  • →Log data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages — collected automatically.
  • →Anonymous usage statistics via Google Analytics (page visits, device info, interaction data).

🧮 All calculations happen entirely in your browser. We never see or store your calculation inputs or results.

How we use your information

We use the collected information only for:

  • →Operating and maintaining StudentCalc.online.
  • →Improving user experience and website performance.
  • →Responding to user inquiries and support requests.
  • →Analyzing usage trends and preventing fraudulent activity.

Cookies & Google advertising cookies

StudentCalc uses cookies to enhance user experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help improve website functionality.

StudentCalc uses Google AdSense to display advertising, and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including cookies used by Google Analytics and Google Ads) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites, and to measure how our site is used. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings, and you can learn more about how Google uses data in the link below.

Advertising partners

Our advertising partners may use technologies such as cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in advertisements and links that appear on StudentCalc. These technologies are used to measure advertising effectiveness and personalise ad content. StudentCalc has no control over cookies used by third-party advertisers.

Third-party privacy policies

This Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of third-party ad servers or websites for detailed information about their practices and how to opt out.

Your data protection rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • →Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • →Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • →Request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • →Restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  • →Request transfer of your data to another organisation.

If you make a request, we will respond within 7 days.

Children's information (COPPA)

StudentCalc does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will take steps to remove such data.

Frequently asked questions

Does StudentCalc use cookies?

Yes, we use essential session cookies for website functionality, and Google Analytics cookies for anonymous usage statistics.

Is my calculation data stored?

No. All calculations are performed locally in your browser. We never see or store your inputs or results.

How do I delete my contact information?

Contact us through our contact form and we'll permanently delete your information within 48 hours.

Who do I contact about privacy concerns?

Reach us anytime through our Contact page. We take all privacy concerns seriously.
